FRAUD POLICY DOCUMENT — SOP-001
Title: SIM Swap Verification and Escalation Procedure

1. Purpose
This document defines the standard procedure for validating SIM swap requests and
identifying potentially fraudulent SIM swap activity.

2. Risk Indicators
A SIM swap event should be flagged as HIGH RISK when two or more of the following
conditions are met:
  - The swap location is more than 100 km from the customer's registered home city.
  - The stated reason is "lost_phone" but the device used for the swap has not been
    previously registered to the customer.
  - A password reset or financial account login occurs within 2 hours of the swap.
  - The customer has had more than 2 device changes in the last 12 months.

3. Escalation Levels
  - LOW RISK (0 indicators): Auto-approve, no further action.
  - MEDIUM RISK (1 indicator): Send verification SMS/email, hold for 1 hour.
  - HIGH RISK (2+ indicators): Auto-block pending manual verification call to customer
    using the registered emergency contact number. Case must be escalated to the Fraud
    Investigation Team within 30 minutes.

4. Required Actions for HIGH RISK Cases
  - Freeze outbound transactions on the account.
  - Notify the customer via an alternate verified channel.
  - Generate an investigation report referencing this SOP.
