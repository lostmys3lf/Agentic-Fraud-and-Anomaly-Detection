FRAUD POLICY DOCUMENT — SOP-003
Title: Subscription and Promo Bundle Abuse Policy

1. Purpose
This document defines the criteria for identifying customers who exploit promotional
bundle offers through repeated cancel-and-recreate account behavior.

2. Risk Indicators
  - A customer redeems the same or similar promo bundle more than 3 times within a
    90-day period.
  - Promo redemptions are followed by cancellation and re-subscription within 7 days.
  - No corresponding regular usage (voice, data, SMS) is recorded between redemptions.

3. Escalation Levels
  - LOW RISK: 1–2 redemptions in 90 days. No action.
  - MEDIUM RISK: 3 redemptions in 90 days. Flag account, reduce promo eligibility.
  - HIGH RISK: 4+ redemptions in 90 days with cancel-recreate pattern. Block promo
    eligibility for 180 days and open an investigation case.

4. Required Actions for HIGH RISK Cases
  - Revoke promo eligibility for the account.
  - Reverse any promo credit granted in the current cycle if terms were violated.
  - Generate an investigation report and notify the Retention team.
