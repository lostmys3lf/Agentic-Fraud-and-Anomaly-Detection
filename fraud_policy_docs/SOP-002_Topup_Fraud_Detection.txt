FRAUD POLICY DOCUMENT — SOP-002
Title: Topup and Voucher Transaction Fraud Detection

1. Purpose
This document defines risk thresholds for identifying fraudulent topup and voucher
redemption patterns, commonly associated with reseller fraud rings or stolen payment
instruments.

2. Risk Indicators
  - The same payment instrument (e-wallet ID, card number, or device fingerprint) is
    used across 3 or more distinct customer accounts within a 24-hour window.
  - Transactions occur between 01:00 and 05:00 local time in unusually high volume.
  - Transaction amounts cluster near round numbers (e.g., 200,000 / 250,000 / 300,000)
    across many accounts in a short burst.

3. Escalation Levels
  - LOW RISK: No shared instrument detected, normal transaction timing. No action.
  - MEDIUM RISK: Shared instrument found across 2 accounts. Flag for monitoring.
  - HIGH RISK: Shared instrument or device fingerprint found across 3+ accounts within
    24 hours. Suspend the payment instrument and open an investigation case.

4. Required Actions for HIGH RISK Cases
  - Temporarily suspend the shared payment instrument network-wide.
  - Cross-check all linked accounts for other suspicious activity (SIM swaps, promo abuse).
  - Generate a consolidated investigation report covering all linked accounts.
